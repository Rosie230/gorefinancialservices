What we collect

If you contact Gore Financial Services through this website, we may collect your name, email address, business name, service interest, and any other information you choose to include in your message.

How we use that information

We use enquiry information to review your request, respond to you, determine whether our services are a fit, and maintain appropriate business records of client communications.

Hosting and third-party services

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. If the contact form is enabled, submissions may also pass through the configured third-party form provider identified in the site configuration. Those providers may process or store information on infrastructure outside this repository.

Retention and security

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, assess potential engagements, comply with legal obligations, or maintain appropriate business records. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

If you have submitted personal information through this website and would like to request access, correction, or deletion, contact the business using the details that will be published on the live contact page.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time as the website, service providers, or business operations change. The latest published version on this page will apply.
